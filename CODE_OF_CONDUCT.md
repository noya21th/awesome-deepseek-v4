# Code of Conduct

This community exists to help people understand and ship with DeepSeek V4. That's the whole point. Conduct flows from that.

## Short version

- **Be kind.** Software is stressful; people come here to learn or solve a problem. Treat them the way you want to be treated on your worst day.
- **Assume good faith.** The person on the other end of the PR or issue probably isn't trying to waste your time. Ask before accusing.
- **Keep it about the work.** Identity attacks, slurs, harassment, and political sniping unrelated to V4 are not welcome.
- **Credit others.** Benchmark numbers, translations, and analysis cost someone effort. Cite sources. Link to the people who taught you something.

## What we don't tolerate

- Personal attacks, harassment, doxxing, or any conduct a reasonable person would find threatening.
- Discrimination based on nationality, language, gender, race, religion, sexuality, disability, or any other protected characteristic.
- Deliberate misinformation (posting benchmark numbers you know are wrong, fabricating quotes, etc.).
- Off-topic political or nationalist flame wars. V4 is a Chinese-origin model and the project is used globally — that's a strength, not an invitation to fight.
- Commercial spam, SEO-farming, or link-beg that doesn't serve a learner.

## Reporting

If someone violates this code, or if something here needs a judgement call, open an issue titled `[moderation]` or email the repo maintainers (address in the top of the main README once established).

Reports are handled quickly and privately. Retaliation against reporters is itself a violation.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may take any of the following actions, in roughly escalating order:

1. A private note asking someone to change behavior.
2. A public note on the offending thread.
3. Edit or remove the offending content.
4. Temporary block from issues / PRs.
5. Permanent block.

We try to be proportionate and explain ourselves. We're also not a court — judgement calls happen.

## Scope

This applies across all project spaces: issues, PRs, discussions, chat channels (if any), and any venue where someone is participating on behalf of this project.

## One last thing

We are a volunteer project trying to help a global community understand a new open model. Be the kind of person who makes that easier, not harder. Thank you.
